Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview

FluxReviews ("we", "our", or "us") is an independent movie review platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit fluxreviews.netlify.app, how we use it, and your rights. We do not sell your data and we do not run ads.

2. Information We Collect

We collect very minimal information:

  • Likes — when you like a review, a count is incremented in our Firebase database. No personal identifier is stored; we only record that a like occurred, not who gave it.
  • Browser storage — we store liked review IDs in your browser's localStorage. This never leaves your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Usage data — Netlify and Firebase may collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type) governed by their own privacy policies.

We do not collect names, email addresses, passwords, or any personally identifiable information from visitors.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Realtime Database (Google) — stores reviews, OTT updates, and like counts. Firebase Privacy Policy ↗
  • TMDB — we fetch movie metadata and posters via TMDB's public API. We do not share your data with TMDB. TMDB Privacy Policy ↗
  • Netlify — hosts this website. Netlify Privacy Policy ↗
  • Google Fonts — fonts loaded from Google's CDN. Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • Google AdSense — we display ads served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ad Settings ↗. Google Privacy Policy ↗

4. Advertising

FluxReviews uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. These cookies allow Google and its partners to show you ads based on your browsing history.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings ↗.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies via aboutads.info ↗.

5. Cookies

FluxReviews itself does not set tracking cookies. However, our advertising partner Google AdSense may set cookies to serve personalised ads based on your browsing activity. The only first-party storage we use is localStorage (not a cookie) to remember which reviews you have liked.

You can manage or disable advertising cookies via Google Ad Settings ↗ or your browser's cookie settings.

6. Children's Privacy

FluxReviews does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The site is intended for general audiences who enjoy movies and entertainment content.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
